# Brand architecture

## Positioning

**One sentence:** Hope Theory Labs is an AI and software engineering company in Dhaka delivering custom applications, forward-deployed solutions, intelligent workflows and integrated business systems.

**Short description** (bios, profiles): Hope Theory Labs designs and delivers custom software, web/mobile applications, AI integrations, automation and connected enterprise systems. Forward-deployed engineering keeps implementation close to the people who use it; optional AI Audits help teams prioritize opportunities when needed.

**What we are not:** a website shop, an SME accounting startup, a GPU-hosting business or an AI research lab. Those may be things we can do; they are not who we are.

## Structure

Hope Theory Labs is a **single masterbrand**. Everything public carries the HTL identity.

| Level | Name | How it appears |
|---|---|---|
| Masterbrand | Hope Theory Labs | Full lockup. "HTL" after first mention. |
| Service pillars | AI Audit · Forward-Deployed AI Engineering · AI Integration and Automation · Custom Software, Web and Mobile · Enterprise Systems and Data · Managed Engineering | Six descriptive service names, each with a matching icon. No separate service logos. |
| Engagement model | Any service can be engaged independently | Start with a short discovery discussion and scoped proposal; an AI Audit is optional, not mandatory before engineering. |
| Separate website offer | Starter · Counter · Showroom | Standard, published website packages. A website is not required before AI or custom engineering work. |
| Products | HTL ServeAI (pre-production) | "HTL" prefix + product name in Instrument Sans. Uses the masterbrand palette. Always labelled with its real status. |
| Internal tools | MausBot | Not used in public material. |

## Naming rules

- Write **Hope Theory Labs** in full the first time, then **HTL**. Do not write "Hope Theory", "HT Labs", "HTC" or "Hopetheorylabs".
- All caps **HOPE THEORY LABS** only in the wordmark.
- Product names: `HTL` + a plain noun. No new sub-brands without a founder decision.
- Do not include any founder names, portraits, personal bios or team-member identities in public-facing brand material until a separate explicit publication decision.

## Descriptor lines

Use one of these under the logo or in a header, never more than one at a time.

- AI engineering · Custom software · Digital systems · Dhaka
- Software engineering · Applied AI · Integrations
- Tagline: not set (see Voice).
