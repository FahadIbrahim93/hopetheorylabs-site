# Hope Theory Labs — tagline exploration (not approved)

**Status:** Creative exploration only. No new tagline should appear on the public website, metadata, logo or materials until a separate owner selection.

Existing working line: **“Your data. Connected. Your business. Smarter.”** It is familiar but disproportionately emphasizes data connectivity over full-service software and forward-deployed engineering.

| Direction | Candidate | Fit and tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Engineering-led | **From complexity to working systems.** | Broad, practical and memorable; better fits bespoke engineering than AI Audit alone. |
| Deployment-focused | **Beyond prototypes. Into production.** | Strong delivery promise, but only use when completed projects substantiate it. |
| Human + systems | **Build what the work demands.** | Direct and versatile; less explicit about technology. |
| AI + software | **Intelligence, engineered for real work.** | AI-forward but still broad; more abstract. |
| Customer-oriented | **Better systems. Less friction.** | Plain and outcome-focused, but less distinctive. |
| Technology partner | **Software that moves work forward.** | Credible for web, mobile and enterprise; may sound generic. |

## Proposed creative test

Evaluate the top three (From complexity to working systems; Beyond prototypes. Into production; Intelligence, engineered for real work) at: 24px mobile hero eyebrow, desktop brand lockup, LinkedIn profile line and proposal cover. Judge memorability, distinctiveness and match to verified delivery capability. Search trademark and domain similarities before final approval; no clearance has been performed.

**Current decision:** No tagline is approved. Do not replace the current logo geometry or embed these words in public assets without explicit selection.
