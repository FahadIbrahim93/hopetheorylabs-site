# Brand architecture

## Positioning

**One sentence:** a founder-led AI engineering partner that works alongside organisations to design, build, connect and operate custom software and intelligent workflows.

**Short description** (bios, profiles): Hope Theory Labs is a founder-led AI engineering studio building custom software, integrated systems and practical AI for businesses.

**What we are not:** a website shop, an SME accounting startup, a GPU-hosting business or an AI research lab. Those may be things we can do; they are not who we are.

## Structure

Hope Theory Labs is a **single masterbrand**. Everything public carries the HTL identity.

| Level | Name | How it appears |
|---|---|---|
| Masterbrand | Hope Theory Labs | Full lockup. "HTL" after first mention. |
| Service pillars | Forward-Deployed Engineering · Web and Mobile Development · AI Integration and Automation · Enterprise Systems and Data · Managed Engineering | Plain descriptive names in `heading-*` with the matching pillar icon. Never logos of their own. |
| Entry offer | Website packages for small retail | Described in copy only. It is the entry offer, not the brand. |
| Products | HTL ServeAI (pre-production) | "HTL" prefix + product name in Instrument Sans. Uses the masterbrand palette. Always labelled with its real status. |
| Internal tools | MausBot | Not used in public material. |

## Naming rules

- Write **Hope Theory Labs** in full the first time, then **HTL**. Do not write "Hope Theory", "HT Labs", "HTC" or "Hopetheorylabs".
- All caps **HOPE THEORY LABS** only in the wordmark.
- Product names: `HTL` + a plain noun. No new sub-brands without a founder decision.
- Name people in public material only after a founder approves it.

## Descriptor lines

Use one of these under the logo or in a header, never more than one at a time.

- AI engineering · Custom software · Dhaka
- AI and software engineering
- Working tagline (not locked): *Your data. Connected. Your business. Smarter.*
