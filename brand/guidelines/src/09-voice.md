# Brand voice and messaging

## Voice

**Clear, evidence-led, technically capable, direct.** HTL sounds like a senior engineer explaining a plan to a busy owner: calm, specific, honest about what is known and what isn't.

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Plain | Jargon-heavy |
| Specific | Vague or grand |
| Confident | Boastful |
| Honest about status | Inflated |
| Warm and direct | Salesy or urgent |

## Writing principles

1. **Say what it does.** "Connects your orders, stock and accounts into one record", not "unlocks seamless synergy".
2. **Prove it or label it.** Every project is marked Concept, In progress or Shipped. Never invent clients, numbers, reviews, certifications or results.
3. **Short sentences.** One idea each. Active voice.
4. **Sentence case** for headings and buttons. No exclamation marks. No emoji.
5. **Name the outcome first, then the method.** Lead with what changes for the client.
6. **Respect the reader's time.** If a line doesn't help a buyer decide, cut it.

## Messaging hierarchy

1. **What we are:** a founder-led AI engineering partner for organisations.
2. **What we do:** design, build, connect and operate custom software and intelligent workflows.
3. **How we do it:** forward-deployed engineers who work alongside your team until it runs.
4. **Why it matters:** scattered tools become one working record, and routine work gets done by software.

## Approved lines

- Hero: *We engineer software that makes businesses smarter.*
- Working tagline (not locked): *Your data. Connected. Your business. Smarter.*
- Primary call to action: *Discuss a project*
- Secondary call to action: *See our work*
- Brief invitation: *Tell us what your team needs to build, automate or connect.*

## Words and formats

- Company: **Hope Theory Labs**, then **HTL**.
- Prices: `৳10,000` (taka sign, no space, thousands comma). Quote enterprise and custom work on scope; never show the small-retail packages to enterprise buyers.
- Dates: 18 November 2026 in prose; 18 Nov 2026 in labels.
- Avoid: "revolutionary", "cutting-edge", "seamless", "game-changing", "unlock", "leverage", "AI-powered" as a standalone claim, "best-in-class".
- HTL ServeAI is in development. Never describe it as live.

## Examples

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| "AI-powered solutions that transform your business!" | "We connect your tools and add AI where it removes real work." |
| "Trusted by leading brands" | (Say nothing until it's true.) |
| "Get started today — limited slots!" | "Discuss a project" |
| "Our cutting-edge platform" | "The system we built for [client], shipped in [month]." |

## Bangla

Bangla copy follows the same voice: plain, respectful (আপনি form), no slogans translated word for word. It is written or reviewed by a native speaker before publishing.
