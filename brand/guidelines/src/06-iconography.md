# Iconography and illustration

## Pillar icons

The five service pillars have their own icons in **Icons**: `pillar-forward-deployed.svg`, `pillar-web-mobile.svg`, `pillar-ai-automation.svg`, `pillar-systems-data.svg` and `pillar-managed.svg`.

- 24 × 24 grid, 1.5px stroke, square caps, mitred corners, no curves except where a shape needs one.
- The files are drawn in Night `#07090C`. On dark grounds, set the stroke to `ink` (`#EEF0F2`) or inline the SVG with `stroke="currentColor"`.
- Show them at 24px or 32px only, never bigger than 48px. They label; they don't illustrate.

## Interface icons

Use **Lucide** (ISC licence, free for commercial use) for interface icons such as arrows, menus, close, external link and download.

- Stroke width 1.5 to match the pillar icons.
- Size 20px in buttons and fields, 24px elsewhere.
- Colour `ink` or `ink-muted`; `accent` only for the one active item.
- Lucide's rounded caps are softer than the pillar icons. That is acceptable for small interface icons; draw any new brand icon in the pillar style instead.
- No emoji in brand material or the interface.

## Illustration: instrument drawings

HTL does not use stock AI illustration. Instead, explain systems with **instrument drawings**: precise, flat diagrams built from the brand's own parts.

- Boxes are rectangles with 1px `line-control` borders and `radius-none`.
- Connections are straight lines at 0° or 90°, 1.5px, in `ink-muted`. The one active path is `accent`.
- Labels are mono `label` style.
- Measurements and scales use **calibration ticks**: short 1px rules every 8px, every fourth tick longer.
- One Brass signal square marks the outcome or the current state.

Use them for integration maps in proposals, "how it works" sections, case-study architecture and social posts.

## Never use

Glowing brains, robot heads, neural-network webs, particle constellations, circuit-board backgrounds, lens flares, 3D chrome or any image that only says "AI" without showing what the work does.
