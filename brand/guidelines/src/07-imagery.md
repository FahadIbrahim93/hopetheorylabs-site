# Imagery and art direction

## What we show

1. **The work itself.** Real screenshots of software HTL built, shown in a plain browser or device frame on a Deep Ink or Paper ground. Every one carries a status label: Concept, In progress or Shipped.
2. **The places the work serves.** Photographs of real Dhaka working environments: shopfronts, offices, warehouses, building façades and streets. Calm, documentary, natural light.
3. **Instrument drawings.** Diagrams that explain how systems connect (see Iconography).

## Photography direction

- Documentary, not staged. No stock photos of people pointing at screens, handshakes or glowing data.
- Natural or available light. Slightly cool shadows, no heavy filters.
- Texture and honest wear are welcome: worn walls, ledgers, old signage. They show where the work begins.
- Compose with calm negative space so type can sit on it.
- The current site rule is no people in photography. Keep that until there is consent-cleared photography of real clients and the team.
- Only use photographs HTL owns or has a licence for. Record the source and licence of every image.

## Treatment

- Images sit in rectangles with `radius-none` on the page or `radius-lg` inside panels.
- For type over a photo, darken the photo toward Night until the text passes 4.5:1, or put the text on a Deep Ink panel beside it.
- Never tint photos Brass. Never add gradients, glows or particle overlays.

## Honesty rules

- A mockup is labelled "Concept" wherever it appears.
- Never present a mockup, render or generated image as a delivered product, installed hardware or real signage.
- Never show client logos, testimonials or numbers HTL doesn't have.
