## What we sell

1. **Forward-deployed engineering** — work beside the customer's team: workflow, proof of value, integration, production, handoff. On-site time is per project. Not a full-time resident staff. Not gated on buying a website.
2. **Custom web and mobile software** — sites, apps, commerce, SaaS, portals, APIs, internal tools.
3. **AI integration and automation** — LLM apps, retrieval over the customer's own material, agents, document workflows, evaluation, human approval. No published fee.
4. **Enterprise systems and data** — integrations, CRM/ERP edges, pipelines, dashboards, a narrower workflow modernised. No Fortune 500 claim.
5. **Managed engineering** — monitoring, maintenance, small improvements, under a written scope. Not unlimited 24/7 support.

Website packages remain a real, separate offer. They are not the definition of the company, and they are not a ticket required before AI or forward-deployed work.

## Message hierarchy

1. What we do, in one screen.
2. Who it is for: startups and small businesses, growing companies, larger organisations — without pretending we already serve all of them.
3. Problems: unconnected data, manual workflows, outdated software, slow delivery.
4. How: discover, scope, prototype, deploy, improve. Discovery is paid only when agreed. No public discovery fee.
5. Evidence, labelled.
6. How to start: website package, or a custom quote after discovery.

Preferred first screen, still draft:

- Eyebrow: AI engineering · custom software · Dhaka / global delivery
- Heading: We engineer software that makes businesses smarter.
- Supporting line: the exact tagline above.
- Primary action: Discuss your project
- Secondary: Explore our work

Voice: plain, specific, no revolutionary claims, no fake ROI, no invented logos, no countdown.
