# Handling website enquiries

The founder on duty owns enquiry review. Check the private Supabase leads table at the start and end of each Dhaka working day. Before an absence, hand responsibility to the other founder directly. Database receipt works as of the 25 November production check. Email alerts were inactive at that check.

1. In the owner-approved Supabase project, review new entries since the last check. Use the contact channel supplied for the enquiry. Keep personal data inside approved company systems.
2. Record who owns the follow-up and when to check back in private company records. Avoid duplicate replies by checking with the other founder before acting. Never post contact details to GitHub.
3. Each week, review closed and inactive enquiries. The operating default is to remove unanswered or closed prospect records after 90 days without meaningful activity. Record only the date and count of removals. Active work and client records need a separate, documented purpose and review date. This is a manual process.
4. For a deletion request sent to the company inbox, verify control of the original contact channel. Locate the matching enquiry, confirm scope, and remove its active database row and any company-controlled follow-up copies unless a documented obligation requires retention. Check whether email delivery was active for that enquiry. Confirm the outcome to the requester, including any backup or retention limitation. Never request credentials.
5. When a visitor reports a failure, collect the time and displayed message. Check delivery flags and whether the expected destination received it. Offer the WhatsApp path. Keep production retests synthetic, scoped and separately authorised. A provider accepting email does not establish inbox receipt.
